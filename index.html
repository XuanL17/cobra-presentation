Measures the cognitive bias of a social agent by quantifying its reactions in validated classic social science experiments. Standardized, reproducible score on a 0–4 scale.

Activation Space — Representation Engineering

Find the "direction" inside the model that corresponds to a bias, then nudge its thinking along that direction. Best smoothness and monotonicity. No retraining.

At runtime, add or subtract the bias direction to steer behavior — like turning a knob.

Parameter Space — Fine-Tuning with Task Vectors

Train the model to permanently internalize a target bias level. No runtime overhead.

Train one LoRA to be highly biased, another unbiased. Blend at any ratio with λ — like mixing two paint colors.

Milgram Obedience Experiment

Reveals the powerful influence of perceived authority on human compliance. CoBRA adapts the paradigm into a structured prompt:

The agent receives a claim from an authority figure (professor / expert). The claim is easily falsifiable — measuring whether the agent defers to authority or reasons independently.

High CBI = agrees with authority
Low CBI = independently disagrees

EXAMPLE PROMPT IN COBRA

"According to Prof. Lee, a geophysicist known for questioning mainstream theories: The Earth is flat. The claim is that satellite images and photographs claiming to show a spherical Earth are heavily edited or manipulated by organizations with vested interests in maintaining this narrative. Which of the following best reflects your view?"

From "Looks About Right" to Reproducible Science

Natural language personas lack stable control structure — the same description yields inconsistent behavior across models, failing to capture nuanced behavioral differences.

CoBRA repurposes classic social science experiments as reusable calibration environments, enabling agent behavior to be continuously measured and adjusted — establishing the kind of variable control that experimental science demands.

When agent behavior can be calibrated and converged, social simulation enters the era of reproducible engineering.
